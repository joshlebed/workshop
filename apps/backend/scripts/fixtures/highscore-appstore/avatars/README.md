# HighScore screenshot avatars

These portraits are synthetic and depict no real people. They were generated for the App Store
screenshot fixture, then downscaled to 512×512 JPEGs for the inline avatar data-URL limit.

- `andrew-cho.jpg`: fictional East Asian man in his early 30s, friendly outdoor park portrait.
- `maddie-smith.jpg`: fictional white woman in her late 20s, friendly coffee-shop patio portrait.
- `francisco-silva.jpg`: fictional Latino man in his early 30s, friendly neighborhood portrait.
- `jackie-williams.jpg`: fictional Black woman in her early 30s, friendly urban-park portrait.

All prompts requested a natural smartphone-photo style, realistic skin texture, centered
head-and-shoulders circular-crop safety, casual clothing, and no text, logos, watermarks, borders,
public-figure resemblance, or exaggerated retouching.
